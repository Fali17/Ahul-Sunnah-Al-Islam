The Importance of Following the Sunnah

2024-01-15 | Ahlu-Sunnah Al Islam

In the name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful.

The Sunnah of the Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him) is the second source of Islamic legislation after the Quran. Following the Sunnah is essential for every Muslim who seeks guidance and success in this life and the next.

Allah says: 'Indeed in the Messenger of Allah you have a good example to follow...' [33:21]

Why Sunnah is Important

1. It explains the Quran
2. It provides practical examples
3. It is preserved and authentic
